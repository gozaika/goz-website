# Creative Review Checklist

Status: required for every still, store asset, website loop, and app preview video.

## Scoring

Score each criterion from 1 to 5.

- 1 = fails the launch bar.
- 2 = functional but visibly weak.
- 3 = acceptable baseline, not premium.
- 4 = polished and credible.
- 5 = launch-grade, category-leading.

Codex must provide the first score. The owner may downscore or upscore after review. An asset cannot
be called launch-grade unless every v3 criterion is 4 or 5 and there are no open truth, readability,
copy-leakage, or AI-artifact blockers.

## Required Passes

| Pass | Meaning | Exit bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v1 functional | Correct screenshot, crop, dimensions, route, surface, and copy boundary. | No truth blockers. |
| v2 visually polished | Brand tokens, device mockup, shadows, gradients, composition, and background are refined. | Average score >= 4 with no score below 3. |
| v3 launch-grade | Final export tuned against premium peer benchmarks and owner feedback. | Every score >= 4; owner accepts or explicitly waives. |
